# FTC: Nihon - Schedule Evaluation

> A simplified view to assess daily pacing, travel burden, and capacity for first-time Japan visitors with children.

---

## Intensity Scale

| Level | Meaning | Recommendation |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| 🟢 Light | 1-3 activities, minimal transit | Perfect pacing |
| 🟡 Moderate | 4-5 activities, some transit | Good but stay flexible |
| 🟠 Full | 5-6 activities, significant transit | Cut 1-2 items if tired |
| 🔴 Heavy | 6+ activities, lots of transit | Likely too ambitious |

### Key Recommendations

1. **Day 3 needs trimming** - Cut animal cafe AND Kabukicho OR replace with Shibuya dinner
2. **Day 7 pick one path** - Either Open-Air Museum focus OR Owakudani + Shrine route
3. **Day 9 pace Fushimi Inari** - First viewpoint only with kids
4. **Day 10 Golden Pavilion decision** - Move to Day 11 morning or cut rickshaw to 1 hr
5. **Day 14 leave Nara buffer** - Taxi backup plan if running late for Sumo

### Transit Tips for Japan Newcomers

| Challenge | Reality | Tip |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| Station navigation | Stations are HUGE and confusing | Add 10-15 min buffer for finding platforms |
| Train timing | Trains are punctual to the second | Miss it by 30 sec = wait for next one |
| IC Cards | Suica/Pasmo are essential | Load ¥5,000+ on arrival, tap everywhere |
| Rush hour | 7:30-9:30 AM is PACKED | Start after 9 AM when possible |
| Walking | Everything involves stairs | Factor in kid pace, not adult pace |
| Google Maps | Works well in Japan | Download offline maps for each city |

### Capacity Buffer Rule

> **With 3 kids ages 7-10, plan for 70% of what you think you can do.**

If schedule says 5 activities, realistically complete 3-4 well.
Better to have "bonus time" than stressed rushing.
